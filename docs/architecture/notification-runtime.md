## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-1 | **Module shape.** `Features/Notifications` with `Contracts` and internal services; entities in `Domain/Notifications`; tables in `notifications` exactly as ERD §5.18 draws them (`NotificationSchemaTests`). Notifications references only `Application/Common` and the IdentityAccess and MasterDataConfig contracts (E-U1, E-U2); architecture tests A-1 to A-6 pass with `ModuleRegistry` unchanged. | ADR-003 §4.3; M-13. |
| D-2 | **Commit before notify, by construction.** A producer stages its intent in `common.outbox_message` with `IOutbox`, inside its own transaction; the dispatcher reads only committed rows, so an intent whose source is still open, or was rolled back, is never seen. The intent contract — `NotificationIntentData`, `NotificationParameter`, `NotificationCondition`, `NotificationIntentEnvelope` — is in `Application/Common/Events`, so E-U4 needs no module reference (as `ModuleRegistry` already states). The dispatcher now delivers NOTIFICATION_INTENT messages as well as DOMAIN_EVENTs: every intent to the one `INotificationIntentConsumer`, in the transaction that marks the message dispatched, with EV-6's five attempts and back-off. | Acceptance criterion 1; EV-6; event-conventions §3 ("Notifications, only"). |
| D-3 | **Intake only records.** `NotificationIntentIntake` checks EV-10 and stores the intent RECEIVED, or SCHEDULED for a reminder not yet due, with its parameters. The unique (source event type, source reference) makes a repeated delivery a no-op (EV-4). An intent that breaks the contract — a deep link that is not a route (a scheme, a host, a query string, no leading slash), a source reference other than the idempotency key, a message key other than `<eventType>:<idempotencyKey>`, a reminder without a condition, a malformed or duplicate parameter, a parameter named `deepLink` — is refused with an exception: the dispatcher keeps the message, retries it and finally leaves it with its `last_error` (EV-6's dead letter), and nothing is half-recorded. | EV-3, EV-4, EV-10. |
| D-4 | **A failed delivery cannot reach the source.** The source committed before the intent was even received. Routing and each delivery attempt run later, each in its own transaction on a row claimed with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, so two API instances never route or send the same row. A pass that cannot handle an intent (a condition source or the database down) rolls it back, logs the fault's type and moves the intent to the back of the queue. | Acceptance criterion 2; the workbook's first validation check. |
| D-5 | **Reminders revalidate when due.** A reminder is an intent with `scheduledFor` and a `condition { subjectType, subjectId, satisfiedWhenStatusIn[] }` (EV-10). It stays SCHEDULED until due; then Notifications asks the source's `INotificationConditionSource` — chosen by the envelope's `sourceModule` — for the subject's status now. A status outside the set, or no such subject, suppresses it (`CONDITION_RESOLVED`); no condition source for the module suppresses it too (`CONDITION_UNVERIFIABLE`): a reminder is never sent unverified. `condition_revalidated_at` records the check. The seam is in `Application/Common/Events`, implemented by the source and called by Notifications, so neither references the other and ADR-003 §8.2 gains no Notifications → source query edge: event-conventions S-7 is resolved without the edge it proposed. | Acceptance criterion 3; the workbook's second validation check; S-7. |
| D-6 | **Recipients are resolved from the matrices, never named by the source.** Routing resolves the NOTIFICATION_ROUTING version in force now and takes the intent's family: its recipient roles, its channels and whether it is mandatory (ADR-004's three matrices, TASK-034). `IRoleHolderDirectory`, a new IdentityAccess contract (E-U1), returns the holders of those roles over the envelope's scope: an active person with an assignment in force, bound to a PUBLISHED profile version of one of the roles, each anchor the assignment carries (project, department, entity) equal to the intent's. An external person counts only through R04 or R08, only while their entity is active, and only for a record of their own entity — the engine's ADR-013 bounds (`authorization-engine.md` D-3). So an entity Project Manager, whose assignment is bound to their project, is notified in-app and by e-mail on that project and on no other (ADR-013). No routing in force, or no such family: FAILED `CONFIGURATION_MISSING`. No holder: SUPPRESSED `NO_ELIGIBLE_RECIPIENT`. | EV-10 ("recipients are never named in the intent"); participation amendment. |
| D-7 | **The recheck happens again at every send attempt.** A retry can come hours after routing, so before an e-mail or SMS attempt the recipient must still be a holder over the scope, still active, still not opted out, and — for SMS — still have a verified number; otherwise the delivery is SUPPRESSED (`RECIPIENT_INELIGIBLE`, `RECIPIENT_OPTED_OUT`, `MOBILE_UNVERIFIED`). Rendered content is plain text on every channel; a parameter is substituted as text and cannot become markup or a header (a subject's line breaks are removed). The operations views show where a notification went, never what it said. | "Eligibility/privacy recheck" (workbook); EV-3. |
| D-8 | **Templates are bilingual, mandatory and governed.** One template per event type and channel, in both languages always (ADR-012); e-mail and in-app have a subject in both, SMS none. They follow the governed lifecycle primitive: the author drafts and alone edits, a second person validates, a third publishes (ERD D-12). Publishing retires the version it replaces in the same save; routing renders the PUBLISHED version, the highest-numbered if two publications raced. A family routed to a channel with no PUBLISHED template for the event type sends nothing on any channel: FAILED `TEMPLATE_MISSING`, redriven once the template exists. A template naming a parameter the intent lacks: FAILED `TEMPLATE_PARAMETER_MISSING`. Each recipient's copy is rendered in their preferred language and stored as rendered, so history shows what was sent after the template changes. Placeholders are `{{name}}`; `{{deepLink}}` is the intent's route (absolute, on `APP_BASE_URL`, for e-mail and SMS). | ADR-012 (gate cell); ERD `notification_template`, `rendered_body` (derived register row 23). |
| D-9 | **Channels.** *In-app*: stored SENT when routed; it is the inbox. *E-mail*: `SmtpEmailSender` hands a plain-text UTF-8 message, with the recipient's `Content-Language`, to the Exchange relay over one connection per message. TLS is required — implicit on port 465, STARTTLS otherwise, and a relay that does not offer it is refused — unless `Notifications:Email:UseTls` is false. *SMS*: `ISmsGateway`; no provider is selected, so `UnconfiguredSmsGateway` reports the channel not configured (F-2). A channel whose adapter or `APP_BASE_URL` is missing is SUPPRESSED `CHANNEL_NOT_CONFIGURED` for that delivery; the other channels still deliver. | Deliverable "In-App + Email channel adapters"; ADR-004. |
| D-10 | **SMS carries the event and its deep link only, within the budget.** An SMS template's only placeholder is `{{deepLink}}`, and it must contain it; no parameter can reach an SMS, so no sensitive value can either (TASK-083's rule). Segments are counted as the network counts them: GSM 7-bit 160 septets, 153 per concatenated segment, an extension character two; any other character — every Arabic letter — puts the text in UCS-2, 70 characters, 67 per concatenated segment. At authoring each language must fit `SmsMaxSegments` (3) with the link counted at `SmsDeepLinkAllowance` (130): an Arabic text of up to 71 characters. At routing the real link is counted and stored in `segment_count`; an SMS over budget is SUPPRESSED `SMS_SEGMENT_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, never cut. SMS goes only to a number its holder verified (`IUserContactDirectory`, TASK-031). | ADR-004 as changed; TASK-103 "enforce that SMS templates carry event plus deep link only". |
| D-11 | **Preferences: on by default, recipient opt-out, mandatory families fixed.** A channel of a family starts as the matrix's `enabled_by_default`. A recipient may turn e-mail or SMS of a non-mandatory family on or off; in-app is always on, and every channel of a mandatory family keeps the matrix's setting whatever is stored — including a choice made before the family became mandatory. `PUT /notification-preferences` applies all changes or none: a family not in the routing in force, or a channel it is not routed to, is 422 `NOTIFICATION_PREFERENCE_INVALID`; in-app or a mandatory family is 422 `NOTIFICATION_PREFERENCE_NOT_CONFIGURABLE`. Routing and every send attempt read the stored choice, so a change applies to the next send. A change is audited. | ADR-004; TASK-040's acceptance criterion ("honored by the WF-15 backend's channel routing on the next send"). |
| D-12 | **The inbox is the caller's own.** Every inbox query and change is filtered by the caller's id in SQL; another person's notification is 404 like an unknown id (R-47). Reading a notification is SENT → READ once; mark-all-read marks what was sent until then, so only a new notification makes the badge count again. History lists every delivery to the caller on every channel with its outcome. These endpoints declare `[AllowAnyAuthenticatedUser]`, and reading and preferences declare the new `[NonSensitiveWrite]`, the R-35 classes "marking a notification read" and "user preferences". | TASK-040's acceptance criteria; R-35, R-47. |
| D-13 | **Retry, dead letter and redrive.** A failed attempt leaves the delivery FAILED with `next_attempt_at` after `RetryBaseDelay` × 2^(attempt − 1) (1, 2, 4, 8 minutes); the `MaxAttempts`-th failure makes it DEAD_LETTER with `dead_lettered_at`. `failure_reason` is the fault's type only, never a provider's text, which can echo an address. A routed intent becomes COMPLETED when no delivery is PENDING or FAILED. An operator redrives a DEAD_LETTER delivery (PENDING, attempts reset; its intent reopens) or a FAILED intent (RECEIVED); both are PRIVILEGED_ACTION audit events, and any other state is 409 `INVALID_TRANSITION`. | Deliverable "retry/dead-letter handling". |
| D-14 | **Four permissions, group `NOTIFICATIONS`, to R01 at ALL** (the user's decision, 2026-09-30, as FG-04's): `NOTIFICATION_TEMPLATE_VIEW`, `NOTIFICATION_TEMPLATE_MANAGE`, `NOTIFICATION_DELIVERY_VIEW`, `NOTIFICATION_DELIVERY_MANAGE`. A person's own inbox and preferences need none. | TASK-030's rule; F-1. |
| D-15 | **History is fixed in the database** (migration `TASK-039_GuardNotificationHistory`). No template, intent, parameter or delivery is deleted. A template is born DRAFT, moves only forward, and its text changes only while DRAFT. An intent's source, scope, schedule and link never change; SUPPRESSED is final; FAILED returns only to RECEIVED. A parameter never changes. A delivery's recipient, channel, template and rendered content never change; it is born PENDING (e-mail, SMS), SENT (in-app) or SUPPRESSED, moves only along the runtime's transitions, and DELIVERED, READ and SUPPRESSED are final. | "History" (workbook); as TASK-035 and TASK-037 did. |
| D-16 | **Three migrations, one schema each** (migrations README R-7): `TASK-039_CreateNotificationTables`, `TASK-039_AddNotificationCrossModuleForeignKeys`, `TASK-039_GuardNotificationHistory`. I-46 and I-47 are built; the worker's two queues (partial indexes on intents to route and deliveries to send) and the operations list are the module's own. Five unique keys whose generated names exceed 63 characters are named. | TASK-024 conventions; `indexing-strategy.md` §1. |
| D-17 | **A service principal** `svc.notification-dispatch` (`…00f9`, `NotificationServicePrincipal.Id`) authors every receipt, routing, attempt and completion, so each row has a `created_by`/`updated_by` (ERD D-2). | ERD D-2. |
| D-18 | **`NotificationChannel` moved to `Domain/Common`.** FG-04's matrix and WF-15's deliveries both hold it; in `Domain/MasterDataConfig` it failed `DomainTypesHoldNoStateOfAnotherModule` (A-4). Only its namespace changed. | A-4. |
| D-19 | **Audited through `IAuditTrail`**, in the saving transaction: `TemplateCreated`, `TemplateUpdated`, `TemplateValidated`, `TemplatePublished`, `TemplateRetired` (CONFIGURATION_CHANGE, with the text, which is configuration); `PreferenceChanged` (DATA_CHANGE); `IntentRedriven`, `DeliveryRedriven` (PRIVILEGED_ACTION). Routing and delivery are operational history in the module's tables, not audit events; rendered content is never copied to the audit store. | event-conventions row 15; CTL-25. |

## 4. Codes

`NotificationErrorCodes` (R-27): `NOTIFICATION_TEMPLATE_INVALID` (422, the fields carry `REQUIRED`, `NOT_ALLOWED`, `MALFORMED_PLACEHOLDER`, `SMS_CONTENT_RESTRICTED` or `SMS_SEGMENT_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`), `NOTIFICATION_SEPARATION_OF_DUTIES` (422), `NOTIFICATION_TEMPLATE_VERSION_CONFLICT` (409), `NOTIFICATION_PREFERENCE_NOT_CONFIGURABLE` (422), `NOTIFICATION_PREFERENCE_INVALID` (422).

`suppression_reason` (`NotificationSuppressionReasons`). On an intent: `CONDITION_RESOLVED`, `CONDITION_UNVERIFIABLE`, `NO_ELIGIBLE_RECIPIENT` (SUPPRESSED); `CONFIGURATION_MISSING`, `TEMPLATE_MISSING`, `TEMPLATE_PARAMETER_MISSING` (FAILED). On a delivery: `RECIPIENT_OPTED_OUT`, `CHANNEL_OFF_BY_DEFAULT`, `CHANNEL_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `MOBILE_UNVERIFIED`, `SMS_SEGMENT_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, `RECIPIENT_INELIGIBLE`.

## 5. How a module notifies

1. **Declare the event type** in your `Contracts/Events`: a record deriving from `EventEnvelope<NotificationIntentData>` (EV-8), named after the occurrence (EV-1), e.g. `ConcernEscalated`.
2. **Stage it in your own transaction** with `IOutbox.Stage`, beside the change it reports, before your save: kind NOTIFICATION_INTENT, `idempotencyKey` = `data.sourceReference` = your occurrence's id (EV-4), `messageKey` = `<eventType>:<idempotencyKey>`, `scope` = the record's project, department and entity (M-7), `data.deepLink` = the SPA route, `data.parameters` = what the templates name, nothing sensitive (EV-3). Never name a recipient.
3. **For a reminder**, set `data.scheduledFor` and `data.condition` (subject type and id, the statuses in which it still matters), and register an `INotificationConditionSource` for your module that reads the subject's status now. Without it your reminders are suppressed `CONDITION_UNVERIFIABLE`.
4. **Ask FG-04 and a template author** for the family (its channels, recipient roles, mandatory flag, in the NOTIFICATION_ROUTING version) and for PUBLISHED templates on each routed channel, in both languages. Without them your intents are FAILED and can be redriven once they exist.
