The foreign keys `project_baseline.change_authorization_id` and `financial_commitment.change_authorization_id` TASK-046 and TASK-052 left for this task are added | ERD §5.11 |
| D-3 | **The state machine** (§3) is the row's and the ERD's: twelve edges in `ChangeRequestWorkflow`. The requester `submit`s (DRAFT, or RETURNED as the next revision, → SUBMITTED) and `withdraw`s (SUBMITTED, RETURNED → WITHDRAWN). **AHDA starts the review** (`start-review`, SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW), which records the materiality and starts the WF-11 run in one transaction — TASK-041 D-3's reading of Submitted and Under Review, and the amendment's "materiality and approval remain AHDA's". WF-11's outcome takes UNDER_REVIEW to APPROVED, RETURNED, REJECTED or WITHDRAWN. AHDA `start-implementation` (APPROVED → IMPLEMENTATION), `mark-implemented` (→ IMPLEMENTED, every authorisation applied) and `close`s (→ CLOSED). REJECTED, WITHDRAWN and CLOSED are final (BR-CHG-015): any command is 409 `TERMINAL_STATE`; a step not in the machine is 409 `INVALID_TRANSITION`. Fields change while DRAFT or RETURNED only (409 `CHANGE_REQUEST_NOT_EDITABLE`); only a DRAFT is deleted (HARD_DRAFT, BR-CHG-037) | Row: "Draft to Submitted to Under Review to Returned/Approved/Rejected to Implementation to Implemented to Closed"; ERD §6 row 22 |
| D-4 | **Materiality, the deliverable "materiality rules".** `ChangeMateriality`, ADR-016's rule over the three bands of the project's governance profile in the MATERIALITY_BAND version in force (`RequireMaterialityBands`): (1) a dimension applies only when the request states an impact on it — cost, schedule, scope — and its band is otherwise null, not 1; (2) cost and schedule are judged on the **absolute cumulative position**: the impacts of the project's approved requests (APPROVED, IMPLEMENTATION, IMPLEMENTED, CLOSED) whose evaluation was against the same ACTIVE Approved Baseline, plus this request's — a reduction is a change too; (3) a band's absolute and percentage thresholds are independent alternatives: cost against SAR or a percentage of the ACTIVE Approved Budget, schedule against days or a percentage of the baseline's duration in calendar days, from the earliest planned start it froze to its finish; (4) scope escalates by rule: a request with a scope impact takes the highest band naming a scope rule (F-6); (5) a dimension reaching no threshold is band 1; (6) a contractual obligation is band 3 (TASK-106); (7) the result is the highest. Generic: no threshold or rule code is named in code (OQ-013). `MaterialityEvaluator` reads the commitments as they stand — the ACTIVE Approved Baseline through `IApprovedBaselineSource`, the ACTIVE Approved Budget through `IApprovedBudgetSource` (D-10) — and **fails closed**: a schedule impact without an ACTIVE Approved Baseline, or a cost impact without an ACTIVE Approved Budget, is 422 `CHANGE_REQUEST_TARGET_UNAVAILABLE` (missing is never zero, BR-CHG-031); no version, or no three bands for the profile, 422 `CONFIGURATION_MISSING`. **Recorded once per revision**, at `start-review`: the evaluation row pins the version, the baseline and budget evaluated against with their versions and bases, the cumulative figures and every band (ERD §7 row 14), so a later publication changes nothing recorded. **Previewed before** (`POST …/preview-materiality`, advisory, recorded nowhere) so the requester sees the approval path before submitting (TASK-061's acceptance criterion; WF-08 §6.1 "Draft results are advisory"); after the review starts it is 409 `CHANGE_REQUEST_EVALUATED`. **Never input**: no request model has a band field, and an unknown property is ignored | Deliverable; ADR-016; WF-08 §6, CHG-GP-08, BR-CHG-004 |
| D-5 | **Review through WF-11 (edges 22, 28).** `start-review` starts a run for the request's revision, routing key `CHANGE_REQUEST` (the ERD's APPROVAL_AUTHORITY code), scope anchors the project and its department, routing inputs the governance profile, **the resulting band** (APPROVAL_AUTHORITY `band_no`) and the absolute cost impact (`min_amount_sar`). **The run's requester is the originator**, whoever starts the review, so WF-11 keeps them from approving their own change (CHG-GP-06, BR-CHG-016) and gives an external originator no authority (ADR-013); they also withdraw it, which withdraws the request (WF-08 §8 "Withdraw"). `ChangeRequestApprovalOutcomeHandler` applies an outcome only to the revision under review while UNDER_REVIEW, otherwise audits it as ignored (EV-5) | TASK-035; WF-08 §8 |
| D-6 | **Issuance — the deliverable "ChangeAuthorization issuance API".** On APPROVED the handler issues one authorisation per governed commitment the approved revision changes, pinned to the version its evaluation was against (VAL-CHG-015, -016): REBASELINE of the ACTIVE Approved Baseline when the request has a schedule impact (target `Schedule`/`ProjectBaseline`, its id and `version_no`), COMMITMENT_CHANGE of the ACTIVE Approved Budget when it has a cost impact (`FinancialKpi`/`FinancialCommitment`). Each is ISSUED, with the issuance key `ChangeRequest:{id}:r{revision}:{SCOPE}` under the ERD's unique `idempotency_key` — derived from WF-08's own aggregate (R-39) — so no redelivery issues one twice. It is saved after APPROVED, which is when the database accepts one. A scope or governance-profile change issues none (F-4). People read them at `GET /change-authorizations?projectId=&changeRequestId=&authorizationScope=&status=` and `/{id}`, and on the request; nothing issues or applies one over HTTP | Deliverable; ERD `change_authorization`; WF-08 §8 "Final approval creates a scoped ChangeAuthorization" |
| D-7 | **Exactly once — acceptance criterion 1.** A target module presents an authorisation only through `IChangeAuthorizations` (ChangeRequest's `Contracts`): `CheckAsync` when it accepts a change that takes effect later, `ApplyAsync` as the change takes effect. `ChangeAuthorizationRules` decides, in order: it exists; it is of the project and the kind of change claimed (`OutOfScope`); if APPLIED, the same use again — the same `applied_reference` — is `Replayed` and changes nothing, any other is `Consumed`; EXPIRED, REVOKED or past `expires_at` is `Ended`; the target as it stands is the one it pins, id and version (`TargetMoved`); its request is in IMPLEMENTATION (`NotImplementing`). `ApplyAsync` then stages ISSUED → APPLIED with `applied_at`, `applied_by_user_id` and `applied_reference` — **the application's idempotency key, derived from the target module's own record** (`Schedule.ProjectBaseline:{id}`, R-39) — and the audit event `ChangeRequest.ChangeAuthorizationApplied`, **in the target module's unit of work** (M-11): they commit with the change or not at all. Three layers hold "once": the rule (a second use is `Consumed`), the authorisation's row version (two applications racing: the second save fails), and the guard (an APPLIED authorisation never changes). A client retrying the request that applies one — the same rebaseline submission, with the same `Idempotency-Key` — finds the change made and is refused, and nothing changes again (§7); R-37's stored-response replay waits for `common.idempotency_record` (F-7) | Acceptance criterion 1; validation check 1; api-conventions R-39 |
| D-8 | **Approval never changes a target — acceptance criterion 2.** The outcome handler writes `change_request` rows only and calls no target module. Its authorisations apply only once AHDA has opened the implementation (`start-implementation`): an APPROVED request's are `NotImplementing`, and the database refuses an application while the request is not in IMPLEMENTATION, whoever writes (D-13). The change is the target module's own explicit step — a rebaseline on WF-03, a new Approved Budget version on WF-14 — under that module's rules and approval, and its application is audited `ChangeRequest.ChangeAuthorizationApplied` by the person who made the change, apart from `ChangeRequest.ChangeRequestApproved` by the approver | Acceptance criterion 2; validation check 2; CHG-GP-01, BR-CHG-019 |
| D-9 | **The target modules (edges 11, 12).** **WF-03**: every rebaseline after the first APPROVED baseline names an authorisation (BR-SCH-034). `RebaselineAuthorization`, WF-03's typed adapter, replaces `IRebaselineAuthorization` and `NoRebaselineAuthorization` (deleted): the claim is a REBASELINE of the ACTIVE Approved Baseline at its version. Where the governance profile requires baseline approval, submission checks it and WF-11's approval applies it as the candidate activates, the approver as actor; where it does not, submission applies it as the candidate activates. Refused: 422 `SCHEDULE_CHANGE_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED` (unchanged); an approved candidate whose authorisation no longer applies is returned, not activated (`REBASELINE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`, unchanged). **WF-14**: any change to an ACTIVE Approved Budget names one: `POST /financial-commitments/{id}/submit` takes an optional `{changeAuthorizationId}` (the project's first budget needs none). `CommitmentChangeAuthorization` checks it on submission; WF-11's approval applies it as the version activates; refused, 422 `FINANCIAL_CHANGE_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED` (new), and an approved version whose authorisation no longer applies is returned, reason `CHANGE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. Both modules record the authorisation's id on the row they create (ERD), now under a foreign key | `schedule-baseline.md` F-1; `financial-kpi.md` F-3 |
| D-10 | **No cycle.** The evaluation needs the ACTIVE Approved Baseline and Budget, but Schedule and FinancialKpi already reference ChangeRequest (edges 11, 12): a reference back would close a cycle. So ChangeRequest owns two ports in its `Contracts`, `IApprovedBaselineSource` and `IApprovedBudgetSource`, which Schedule (`ApprovedBaselineSource`) and FinancialKpi (`ApprovedBudgetSource`) implement: the code references run Schedule → ChangeRequest and FinancialKpi → ChangeRequest only, the edges ADR-003 already has. ADR-003 §8.2 rows 11 and 12 are rewritten to say what crosses (F-14). A-1 to A-6 pass unchanged | ADR-003 §8.3 "0 cycles"; A-6 |
| D-11 | **ADR-013: raise, not decide or implement.** Four permissions, group `CHANGE_REQUEST`: `CHANGE_REQUEST_VIEW`, `CHANGE_REQUEST_RAISE` (create, edit, delete a draft, submit, withdraw), `CHANGE_REQUEST_REVIEW` (start-review), `CHANGE_REQUEST_IMPLEMENT` (start-implementation, mark-implemented, close). Shipped: R04 view and raise at OWN — the projects the holder manages, internal or entity (the amendment) — and implement at OWN (WF-08 §3: the Project Manager coordinates implementation); R03 view and review at DEPT (§3: the Department Manager reviews). Review and implementation are refused to an external user whatever they hold — a check on the person, since R04 is held by internal and external users alike — and audited `ChangeRequest.AuthorityRefused`, reason `EXTERNAL_USER`. Materiality is the server's computation from AHDA's published bands, never an input; approval is WF-11's `APPROVAL_DECIDE`, which no external user holds | Participation amendment |
| D-12 | **What a request names, and when** (`ChangeRequestReferences`). Raised, edited, submitted and reviewed while the project is APPROVED_PLANNED, ACTIVE or SUSPENDED (review may continue during a suspension, XWA-CHG-001); implementation opened only while APPROVED_PLANNED or ACTIVE (BR-CHG-040); marked implemented and closed until the project is CLOSED (422 `CHANGE_REQUEST_PROJECT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`). On submission it holds what its type needs — SCHEDULE its days, COST its amount, SCOPE its scope impact, CONTRACTUAL_OBLIGATION the flag, GOVERNANCE_PROFILE the profile (422 `CHANGE_REQUEST_INCOMPLETE`) — and the commitments it changes exist (422 `CHANGE_REQUEST_TARGET_UNAVAILABLE`). A stated impact is never zero (400 `OUT_OF_RANGE`): no impact is left out, so a zero never issues an authorisation. A requested profile is a PUBLISHED GOVERNANCE_PROFILE item other than the project's (422 `CHANGE_REQUEST_PROFILE_INVALID`). A request may state impacts on several dimensions: a schedule change with a cost consequence is one decision (BR-CHG-001) and is issued both authorisations | WF-08 §3.1, §12.1 |
| D-13 | **The database holds the history and the criteria** (migration `TASK-060_GuardChangeRequestHistory`). A request is born DRAFT at revision 1, never submitted; keeps its project, type and requester; moves only along the twelve edges; raises its revision by one exactly when a RETURNED request is resubmitted; changes its fields only while DRAFT or RETURNED; enters UNDER_REVIEW only with an evaluation of the revision, and IMPLEMENTED only with every authorisation APPLIED; once final, nothing changes; only a DRAFT is deleted. An evaluation is appended while its request is SUBMITTED and never changed. An authorisation is born ISSUED, under an APPROVED request, naming the approved WF-11 run of that request; its scope and pinned target never change; ISSUED → APPLIED only while its request is in IMPLEMENTATION; once ended it never changes; it is never deleted. No table is truncated | Acceptance criteria, for any writer |
| D-14 | **API shape (R-2 to R-5, R-21, R-47).** `/change-requests` with `projectId` required (R-3) and the filters `status` and `changeType` (sets), most recently changed first (P-2, I-26); each request carries its latest recorded evaluation and its authorisations. Commands on `/change-requests/{id}/…` answer with the request and honour `If-Match` when sent; `PUT` requires it. `/change-authorizations` with `projectId` required and the filters `changeRequestId`, `authorizationScope` and `status`, most recently issued first — what a target module's screen offers. A request the caller may not see is 404; one they may see but not act on, 403; a collection they may not see is an empty page. The specification's catalogue (§18: `/api/projects/{id}/change-requests`, `/approval-tasks/{id}/…`, `/change-authorizations/verify`) is mapped to these conventions and to WF-11's own endpoints (F-17) | api-conventions |
| D-15 | **Every change is audited** in the saving transaction (event-conventions §4 row 24), all with the request as subject: `ChangeRequestCreated`, `ChangeRequestChanged` (narratives withheld), `ChangeRequestDeleted`, `MaterialityEvaluated` (bands, cumulative figures, pinned version and commitments), `OutcomeIgnored` (DATA_CHANGE); `ChangeRequestSubmitted`, `ReviewStarted` (with the run and band), `ChangeRequestApproved`, `ChangeRequestReturned`, `ChangeRequestRejected`, `ChangeRequestWithdrawn` (by WF-11's decider or the requester), `ChangeAuthorizationIssued` (each, with scope and pinned target), `ImplementationStarted`, `ChangeAuthorizationApplied` (each, by the person who made the change, with the record that applied it), `ChangeRequestImplemented`, `ChangeRequestClosed` (LIFECYCLE_TRANSITION); `AuthorityRefused` (AUTHORIZATION_DENIAL) | event-conventions EV-9; acceptance criterion 2's "independently audited" |

## 3. The change request state machine

| From | To | Command | Permission | Waits for | Writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | DRAFT | `POST /change-requests` | `CHANGE_REQUEST_RAISE` (entity PMs included) | the project APPROVED_PLANNED, ACTIVE or SUSPENDED; a valid requested profile | the request, revision 1 |
| DRAFT, RETURNED | SUBMITTED | `submit` | `CHANGE_REQUEST_RAISE` | complete for its type; its commitments exist | `submitted_at`; from RETURNED, `revision_no` + 1 |
| SUBMITTED | UNDER_REVIEW | `start-review` | `CHANGE_REQUEST_REVIEW`, internal | MATERIALITY_BAND bands for the profile; an APPROVAL_AUTHORITY route | the evaluation, pinned; a WF-11 run for the revision, the originator its requester |
| UNDER_REVIEW | APPROVED | WF-11 approves (edge 28) | WF-11's `APPROVAL_DECIDE` | every stage approved | the authorisations, ISSUED |
| UNDER_REVIEW | RETURNED · REJECTED | WF-11 returns · rejects | as above | — | — |
| UNDER_REVIEW | WITHDRAWN | the originator withdraws the run | WF-11's | — | — |
| SUBMITTED, RETURNED | WITHDRAWN | `withdraw` | `CHANGE_REQUEST_RAISE` | — | — |
| APPROVED | IMPLEMENTATION | `start-implementation` | `CHANGE_REQUEST_IMPLEMENT`, internal | the project APPROVED_PLANNED or ACTIVE | — |
| IMPLEMENTATION | IMPLEMENTED | `mark-implemented` | as above | every authorisation APPLIED by its target module | `implemented_at` |
| IMPLEMENTED | CLOSED | `close` | as above | — | `closed_at` |

An authorisation is ISSUED → APPLIED by its target module through `IChangeAuthorizations`, while its request is in IMPLEMENTATION, once. EXPIRED and REVOKED exist in the ERD and the guard; nothing sets them yet (F-9).

## 5. Error codes

`ChangeRequestErrorCodes` (R-27), beside the platform's `INVALID_TRANSITION`, `TERMINAL_STATE` and `CONFIGURATION_MISSING`:

| Code | Status | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_PROJECT_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | 422 | Raising, editing, submitting or reviewing before approval or once COMPLETED; opening implementation while SUSPENDED or later; finishing once CLOSED |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_INCOMPLETE` | 422 | Submitting without what the change type needs; the miss is a field issue |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_PROFILE_INVALID` | 422 | The requested governance profile is not a PUBLISHED item, or is the project's own |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_TARGET_UNAVAILABLE` | 422 | A schedule impact without an ACTIVE Approved Baseline, a cost impact without an ACTIVE Approved Budget |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_NOT_EDITABLE` | 409 | Editing past DRAFT and RETURNED; deleting a request ever submitted |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_EVALUATED` | 409 | A preview once the review has recorded the materiality |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_UNDER_REVIEW` | 409 | `withdraw` under review: the originator withdraws the WF-11 run |
| `CHANGE_REQUEST_AUTHORIZATION_PENDING` | 409 | `mark-implemented` with an authorisation not yet applied |
| `FINANCIAL_CHANGE_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED` (FinancialKpi) | 422 | A change to an ACTIVE Approved Budget without an applicable authorisation |

1. **TASK-061** builds SCR-105–107 on these 14 operations (done: `change-request-ui.md`): the classification before submission is `preview-materiality`'s (its acceptance criterion); Approved and Implemented are the statuses APPROVED and IMPLEMENTED, with the authorisations between them on the request; materiality is shown, never edited.
2. **WF-03 and WF-14 screens** (`schedule-ui.md` F-5, `financial-kpi-ui.md`) can offer the project's ISSUED authorisations of their kind from `GET /change-authorizations?projectId=&authorizationScope=REBASELINE|COMMITMENT_CHANGE&status=ISSUED` instead of an id typed by hand; until one does, a budget revision from the WF-14 screen is refused 422 (F-11).
3. **TASK-065** guards D-7 and D-8 in CI as its "ChangeAuthorization idempotency" invariant; `ChangeAuthorizationTests` is the starting point.
4. **TASK-105** (governance profile change) and any scope commitment record: issue PROFILE_CHANGE / SCOPE_CHANGE authorisations from `ChangeRequestApprovalOutcomeHandler.Issue` and apply them through `IChangeAuthorizations` (F-4).
5. **Configuration**: an environment needs, before WF-08 works end to end, a published MATERIALITY_BAND version with the three bands of every governance profile in use (OQ-013) and an APPROVAL_AUTHORITY `CHANGE_REQUEST` route, by band where the matrix differs (F-3).
