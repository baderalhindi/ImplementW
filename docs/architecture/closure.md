## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-1 | **Module shape.** `Features/Closure`: `Contracts` (the four service interfaces, DTOs, inputs and pages, `ClosureErrorCodes`, `ClosureApprovalRouting`, `Events`), the services, `CloseoutActivation`, `CloseoutMaintenance`, `CloseoutWorkflow`, `CloseoutEligibility`, `CloseoutReadiness`, `ReadinessPolicy`, `ReadinessRollUp`, `PostProjectObligationRules`, `CloseoutGate`, `CloseoutAccess`, `CloseoutViews`, `CloseoutAudit`, `CloseoutServicePrincipal`, `EventHandlers/CloseoutApprovalOutcomeHandler` and the repository port. Entities in `Domain/Closure`; repository, configurations and the worker in `Infrastructure` | ADR-003 §4.3 row 10 |
| D-2 | **Tables.** ERD §5.13 as `erd.dbml` gives them: `completion_case`, `closure_case`, `readiness_check`, `post_project_obligation`. Beyond the ERD (F-8): `closure_case.completion_case_id` is nullable, for the terminal path; an obligation belongs to a completion case **or** a terminal closure case, exactly one; `readiness_check.blocking_count`; `xmin` on the cases and obligations; partial unique keys of one open case and one EFFECTED case of each kind per project; CHECKs for the state facts. `suspension.active_suspension.end_reason` (`RESUMED` \| `PROJECT_CLOSED`) records how a period ended | ERD §5.13, F-042 to F-045 |
| D-3 | **The case state machine** (§3) is WF-09's, one machine for both kinds (`CloseoutWorkflow`, eleven steps): the requester `submit`s (DRAFT, or RETURNED as the next revision, → SUBMITTED) and `withdraw`s (DRAFT, SUBMITTED, RETURNED → WITHDRAWN); AHDA `start-review`s, which starts the WF-11 run; WF-11's outcome takes UNDER_REVIEW to APPROVED, RETURNED, REJECTED or WITHDRAWN; `activate` takes APPROVED to EFFECTED. REJECTED, WITHDRAWN and EFFECTED are final (409 `TERMINAL_STATE`). Fields, readiness evaluation and waivers change only while DRAFT or RETURNED (409 `CLOSURE_CASE_NOT_EDITABLE`); only a DRAFT with no readiness records or obligations is deleted (409 `CLOSURE_CASE_IN_USE` otherwise). At most one open case of each kind per project (409 `CLOSURE_CASE_ALREADY_OPEN`), held by a partial unique key | WF-10 §5, Appendix A; CLO-CC-05, CLO-CC-06 |
| D-4 | **Completion is never automatic — acceptance criterion 1.** No code path moves a project to COMPLETED but `CloseoutActivation` of an APPROVED completion case. Closure subscribes to no event of WF-02 or WF-04 and no module calls into Closure, so 100% schedule progress or every task COMPLETED changes nothing (BR-CLO-001). The only route is: a case raised (`CLOSEOUT_RAISE`), its readiness evaluated and submitted (the gate, D-5), its review started (`CLOSEOUT_REVIEW`), WF-11's approval (`APPROVAL_DECIDE`), then activation — a separate, separately audited event (`Closure.CaseApproved` by WF-11's last approver; `Closure.CaseEffected` and `Project.ProjectCompleted` by the activator). The database refuses COMPLETED without an EFFECTED completion case and CLOSED without an EFFECTED closure case, at commit (D-11) | Acceptance criterion 1; validation check 1; WF-10 P1, BR-CLO-001, BR-CLO-006 |
| D-5 | **Readiness.** `evaluate-readiness` asks each source module, through a read-only contract, how many of the project's records block a criterion, and appends one `readiness_check` row per criterion — PASS or FAIL, the count and the evaluation time. A waiver is a further row, `WAIVED`, by the waiver's holder with a reason, for a criterion that FAILED in the latest evaluation and is waivable. The roll-up reads the latest evaluation with the waivers recorded since: **READY** (all PASS), **READY_WITH_CONDITIONS** (every FAIL waived), **NOT_READY**, or **INCOMPLETE** (never evaluated). `submit` evaluates afresh and refuses NOT_READY or INCOMPLETE 422 `CLOSURE_BLOCKER_EXISTS`, each failing criterion an error field (`DECISIONS_SETTLED`, …, code `NOT_ALLOWED`); `activate` evaluates again immediately before commit and refuses the same way, leaving the case APPROVED (BR-CLO-034, CLO-CC-25). The criteria (`ReadinessPolicy`): completion checks the ten source criteria — `DECISIONS_SETTLED` (WF-11), `TASKS_DISPOSITIONED` (WF-04), `SCHEDULE_RECONCILED` (WF-03), `MILESTONES_DISPOSITIONED` (WF-03, WF-05), `RISKS_DISPOSITIONED` (WF-06), `ISSUES_DISPOSITIONED` (WF-07), `CHANGES_DISPOSITIONED` (WF-08), `SUSPENSION_REQUESTS_SETTLED` (WF-09), `PROGRESS_REPORTED` (WF-02), `FINANCIALS_SETTLED` (WF-14) — and `OBLIGATIONS_OWNED`; normal closure checks what can still move after Completion — decisions, risks, issues, changes — and `OBLIGATIONS_SATISFIED`; terminal closure checks the ten and `OBLIGATIONS_SATISFIED`. `DECISIONS_SETTLED`, `SUSPENSION_REQUESTS_SETTLED` and the two obligation criteria are never waivable (422 `CLOSURE_CHECK_NOT_WAIVABLE`): each is settled where it lives. Readiness records are append-only, kept with the case, readable at `GET /readiness-checks` | WF-10 §6.1–§6.3, BR-CLO-020 to BR-CLO-024, BR-CLO-034 |
| D-6 | **The activation, edges 8 and 45.** `CloseoutActivation` is the one place a project becomes COMPLETED or CLOSED. It revalidates the project's state (422 `CLOSURE_PROJECT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`) and the readiness (D-5), then stages in one unit of work: the project's transition through **`IProjectCloseoutCommands`** (Project checks its own edge and audits `ProjectCompleted` or `ProjectClosed`, stamping `closed_at`); on the terminal path, the project's open suspension ended with reason `PROJECT_CLOSED` through **`ISuspensionClosureCommands`** (WF-09 audits `SuspensionEnded`); the readiness rows it was revalidated against; the case EFFECTED. On closure it also ends every per-project access assignment (`IProjectAccessLifecycle`, ADR-013). One save commits all or nothing; the project's and the case's row versions fail the save if either moved. **Who activates**: `CloseoutActivationWorker` activates APPROVED cases every `Closure:Activation:PollInterval` as `svc.closeout-activation` (WF-10 §13: "System/service"); `POST …/activate` lets AHDA activate one at once (`CLOSEOUT_ACTIVATE`, internal). A case blocked at activation stays APPROVED and is retried on the next pass; one whose activation fails outright — the database refusing its commit — is rolled back, logged and retried, and the pass goes on to the next case (§7.1 M-21) | WF-10 §5 C07, C12; CLO-CC-11, CLO-CC-12 |
| D-7 | **`ActualProjectCompletionDate`** (ERD F-044) is the requester's, on the completion case: required to submit (422 `CLOSURE_INCOMPLETE`), not after today and not before the project's activation (422 `CLOSURE_COMPLETION_DATE_INVALID`), revalidated at submission. It is applied to nothing — no schedule date, baseline or milestone changes (BR-CLO-011 to BR-CLO-017). The specification defaults it to the activation date; here the person who knows when the works finished states it, and the activation time is kept apart as `effected_at` (F-10) | WF-10 §8, BR-CLO-008 |
| D-8 | **CLOSED is terminal and read-only — acceptance criterion 2.** `ClosedProjectGuard.Refusal(project, permission)` in Project's contracts answers 409 `PROJECT_CLOSED` for any permission whose access mode is Write, on a CLOSED project. Every module's access check applies it after authorization — Progress, Schedule, ProjectTask, Milestone, Risk, ManagementConcern, ChangeRequest, Suspension, FinancialKpi and Closure — so it holds for every write endpoint built on those checks, and for those added later the same way. Project refuses its own commands 409 `TERMINAL_STATE`; WF-12 refuses a document upload, link or change for a closed project's records 409 `DOCUMENT_PROJECT_CLOSED`; FG-03 refuses a new access assignment 422 `IDENTITY_ACCESS_PROJECT_CLOSED` (existing). Reads are unaffected. Background passes skip closed projects: WF-06's acceptance expiry steps past a closed project's acceptances with a keyset cursor, so they never crowd out another project's. **In the database**, `project.guard_project()` refuses any change of a CLOSED project row | Acceptance criterion 2; validation check 2; WF-10 BR-CLO-030 |
| D-9 | **Post-project obligations.** An obligation (warranty, defect liability, handover, …) is recorded against the project's open or effected completion case, or an open terminal closure case (422 `CLOSURE_OBLIGATION_CASE_INVALID`), with an optional owner (an active user, 422 `CLOSURE_OBLIGATION_OWNER_INVALID`) and due date. It moves OPEN → IN_PROGRESS, and from either to SATISFIED, CANCELLED or WAIVED (`waive` is `CLOSEOUT_WAIVE`), which are final (409 `CLOSURE_OBLIGATION_SETTLED`). **It may remain open after Completion**: completion requires only that each open obligation have an owner and a due date (`OBLIGATIONS_OWNED`); obligations are tracked through the COMPLETED project; **closure requires every obligation settled** (`OBLIGATIONS_SATISFIED`), the closure policy. Obligations are never deleted | Description; WF-10 §7, BR-CLO-023 |
| D-10 | **ADR-013: raise, not review, waive or activate.** Five permissions, group `CLOSEOUT`: `CLOSEOUT_VIEW`, `CLOSEOUT_RAISE` (cases, evaluation, submission, withdrawal, obligations), `CLOSEOUT_REVIEW` (start-review), `CLOSEOUT_WAIVE` (waive a criterion or an obligation), `CLOSEOUT_ACTIVATE`. Shipped: R04 view and raise at OWN; R03 view, review and waive at DEPT. `CLOSEOUT_ACTIVATE` ships to no role: the worker activates. Review, waiving and activation are refused to an external user whatever they hold, audited `Closure.AuthorityRefused`, reason `EXTERNAL_USER`. The WF-11 run's requester is the originator, so the originator never approves their own case | ADR-013; WF-10 §3, §13 |
| D-11 | **The database holds the history and the criteria** (migrations `TASK-063_AddProjectCloseoutEdges`, `TASK-063_GuardCloseoutHistory`). `guard_project` gains ACTIVE → COMPLETED, COMPLETED → CLOSED and SUSPENDED → CLOSED, stamps `closed_at`, and refuses any change of a CLOSED row. A case is born DRAFT at revision 1, keeps its project and requester, moves only along the eleven steps, is APPROVED only with the APPROVED WF-11 run of its revision; once final, nothing changes; only a DRAFT is deleted. Readiness rows are never updated or deleted. An obligation moves only along its edges and is never deleted. **At commit** (deferred constraint triggers): a project is COMPLETED or CLOSED only with its EFFECTED case of that kind, and an EFFECTED case only with its project in the state it led to. A suspension period ends `PROJECT_CLOSED` only for a CLOSED project. No table is truncated | Acceptance criteria, for any writer |
| D-12 | **API shape (R-2 to R-5, R-21, R-47).** `/completion-cases` and `/closure-cases` with `projectId` required (R-3) and `status` filter; each case carries its latest readiness and roll-up. `/post-project-obligations?projectId=&status=`, `/readiness-checks?completionCaseId=` or `?closureCaseId=`. Commands answer with the resource and honour `If-Match` when sent; `PUT` requires it. A case the caller may not see is 404; one they may see but not act on, 403 | api-conventions |
| D-13 | **Every change is audited** in the saving transaction: `Closure.CaseCreated`, `CaseChanged`, `CaseDeleted`, `ReadinessEvaluated`, `CheckWaived`, `ObligationCreated`, `ObligationChanged`, `OutcomeIgnored` (DATA_CHANGE); `CaseSubmitted`, `ReviewStarted`, `CaseApproved`, `CaseReturned`, `CaseRejected`, `CaseWithdrawn`, `CaseEffected`, `ObligationTransitioned` (LIFECYCLE_TRANSITION); `AuthorityRefused` (AUTHORIZATION_DENIAL). Project audits `ProjectCompleted` and `ProjectClosed`, naming the case; WF-09 audits `SuspensionEnded` with its end reason | event-conventions EV-9, EV-11 |

## 3. The case state machine

| From | To | Command | Permission | Waits for | Writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | DRAFT | `POST /completion-cases` · `POST /closure-cases` | `CLOSEOUT_RAISE` | completion: the project ACTIVE; closure: COMPLETED with its EFFECTED completion case, or SUSPENDED; no open case of the kind | the case, revision 1 |
| DRAFT, RETURNED | DRAFT, RETURNED | `evaluate-readiness` · `waive-check` | `CLOSEOUT_RAISE` · `CLOSEOUT_WAIVE`, internal | — | readiness rows |
| DRAFT, RETURNED | SUBMITTED | `submit` | `CLOSEOUT_RAISE` | the project's state; date and narrative; a fresh evaluation not NOT_READY | readiness rows; `submitted_at`; from RETURNED, `revision_no` + 1 |
| SUBMITTED | UNDER_REVIEW | `start-review` | `CLOSEOUT_REVIEW`, internal | an APPROVAL_AUTHORITY route `COMPLETION` · `CLOSURE` | a WF-11 run, the originator its requester |
| UNDER_REVIEW | APPROVED · RETURNED · REJECTED · WITHDRAWN | WF-11's outcome (edge 28) | WF-11's | — | nothing else: the project is unchanged |
| DRAFT, SUBMITTED, RETURNED | WITHDRAWN | `withdraw` | `CLOSEOUT_RAISE` | — | — |
| APPROVED | EFFECTED | `activate`, or WF-10's pass as `svc.closeout-activation` | `CLOSEOUT_ACTIVATE`, internal | the project's state; a fresh evaluation not NOT_READY | readiness rows; `effected_at`; the project COMPLETED or CLOSED; terminal: the suspension ended; closure: access assignments ended |

## 4. Endpoints

All paths are under `/api/v1`, tag `Closure`, operation ids `Closure_*`. Every non-2xx answer is the R-23 envelope; every `POST` and `PUT` also answers 400 `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED`/`…_INVALID`; commands honour `If-Match` (412 when stale). `{kind}` is `completion-cases` or `closure-cases`; each has the same eleven operations.

## 5. Error codes

`ClosureErrorCodes` (R-27), beside the platform's `INVALID_TRANSITION`, `TERMINAL_STATE` and `CONFIGURATION_MISSING`; and two outside the module.

| Code | Status | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CLOSURE_PROJECT_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | 422 | A completion case of a project not ACTIVE; a closure case of one neither COMPLETED (with its effected completion case) nor SUSPENDED |
| `CLOSURE_CASE_ALREADY_OPEN` | 409 | A second open case of the same kind for the project |
| `CLOSURE_CASE_NOT_EDITABLE` | 409 | Editing, evaluating or waiving past DRAFT and RETURNED; deleting a case ever submitted |
| `CLOSURE_CASE_IN_USE` | 409 | Deleting a draft with readiness records or obligations: it is withdrawn instead |
| `CLOSURE_CASE_UNDER_REVIEW` | 409 | `withdraw` under review: the originator withdraws the WF-11 run |
| `CLOSURE_INCOMPLETE` | 422 | Submitting without the completion date and narrative, or the closure narrative |
| `CLOSURE_COMPLETION_DATE_INVALID` | 422 | A completion date after today or before the project's activation |
| `CLOSURE_BLOCKER_EXISTS` | 422 | Submitting or activating while a criterion fails and is not waived; each such criterion a field |
| `CLOSURE_CHECK_NOT_FAILED` | 422 | Waiving a criterion that did not fail in the latest evaluation |
| `CLOSURE_CHECK_NOT_WAIVABLE` | 422 | Waiving `DECISIONS_SETTLED`, `SUSPENSION_REQUESTS_SETTLED`, `OBLIGATIONS_OWNED` or `OBLIGATIONS_SATISFIED` |
| `CLOSURE_OBLIGATION_CASE_INVALID` | 422 | An obligation's case is not the project's open or effected completion case, or open terminal closure case |
| `CLOSURE_OBLIGATION_OWNER_INVALID` | 422 | An obligation's owner is not an active user |
| `CLOSURE_OBLIGATION_SETTLED` | 409 | Changing an obligation once SATISFIED, CANCELLED or WAIVED |
| `PROJECT_CLOSED` (`ProjectErrorCodes`) | 409 | Any write, in any module, to a CLOSED project's records |
| `DOCUMENT_PROJECT_CLOSED` (`DocumentErrorCodes`) | 409 | A document uploaded to, linked to or changed on a CLOSED project's records |

## 6. How other modules build on it

1. **New readers** (edges 39–46, and 14, 27): each source module answers a count for its own records and changes none — `IProjectTaskCloseoutReader`, `IScheduleCloseoutReader`, `IMilestoneCloseoutReader`, `IRiskCloseoutReader`, `IConcernCloseoutReader`, `IChangeRequestCloseoutReader`, `ISuspensionCloseoutReader`, `IProgressCloseoutReader`, `IFinancialKpiCloseoutReader`, `IApprovalSettlementReader`. A module whose definition of "settled" changes changes its reader; WF-10 records the answer.
2. **Every module with project records** applies `ClosedProjectGuard.Refusal` in its access check after authorization; a new module does the same (D-8). Background passes skip CLOSED projects as WF-06's does.
3. **WF-09** gains `ISuspensionClosureCommands` and `active_suspension.end_reason`; `suspension.md` F-5's remaining part is §9 F-5 here.
4. **TASK-064** builds the screens on the 31 operations; the case carries its roll-up and latest criteria, so a readiness panel needs no second call. Built 2026-10-08: `suspension-closure-ui.md`.
5. **TASK-065** guards D-4 and D-8 in CI; `ClosedProjectWriteTests` and `ClosureLifecycleTests.NoCompletedTaskOrFullProgressCompletesAProjectWithoutTheApprovedCase` are the starting point.
6. **Configuration**: APPROVAL_AUTHORITY rows for `COMPLETION` and `CLOSURE` before a case can be reviewed (F-3).

## 9. Findings

| # | Finding | Owner | Consequence if unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-1 | **The WF-10 specification's wider model is not built.** It has deliverables and their acceptance, per-item open-item dispositions (each task, risk, issue and change given a disposition on the case), lessons learned, a project outcome entity, handover, contract and document checks, records-management exceptions (BR-CLO-021), capability endpoints and notifications. The row and the ERD (§5.13, four tables) fix two cases, readiness checks, the completion date and obligations, which is what is built; open items are dispositioned in their own modules and the readiness criteria count what remains | PMO / Business Analyst | A completion is reviewed on criterion counts and narrative, not an itemised disposition list |
| F-2 | **Few grants ship** (D-10). The decision is `APPROVAL_DECIDE`, which ships to no one; `CLOSEOUT_ACTIVATE` ships to no one; R02, R05–R08 wait for Blueprint Appendix A. | F-3 | **No route exists.** APPROVAL_AUTHORITY has no `COMPLETION` or `CLOSURE` rows; `start-review` fails closed, 422 `CONFIGURATION_MISSING` | AHDA Business Sponsor / Portfolio Office | No case reaches review |
| F-4 | **Obligations do not outlive Closure.** The specification lets an obligation outlive a CLOSED project (warranty periods running past closure); with CLOSED read-only for MVP, an obligation open at closure could never be satisfied, so the closure policy built is that every obligation is settled first (D-9).
